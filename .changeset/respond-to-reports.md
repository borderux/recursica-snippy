Respond to a Snippy report: open a downloaded report with Snippy's panel open, and each comment gets Mark Resolved and Respond. Resolved comments show a green check and answered ones a blue circle. The panel shows a banner and the collected replies, with Copy Responses to send them back to the reviewer, Paste Responses to load responses copied from the same report, and Delete Responses, and the report's comments with Resolved and Responded badges. Responses are saved in Snippy, and the report file stays unchanged.
